# Sources

The handbook synthesises established craft theory. Primary sources are the
standard works; the links below are the references consulted while compiling it.

## Standard works behind the material

- Robert McKee, *Story* and *Dialogue* — scene as value shift, turning points, subtext
- Dwight V. Swain, *Techniques of the Selling Writer* — scene/sequel, motivation-reaction units
- Blake Snyder, *Save the Cat!* / Jessica Brody, *Save the Cat! Writes a Novel* — the 15 beats
- Shawn Coyne, *The Story Grid* — genre conventions and obligatory scenes
- K.M. Weiland, *Creating Character Arcs* — Ghost/Wound/Lie/Want/Need, the five arc types
- Lisa Cron, *Story Genius* / *Wired for Story* — misbelief, why readers care
- Les Edgerton, *Hooked* — openings and the inciting incident
- John Gardner, *The Art of Fiction* — psychic distance
- Gwen Hayes, *Romancing the Beat* — the romance beat structure
- Dan Harmon — the Story Circle
- John Truby, *The Anatomy of Story* — the ghost, moral argument
